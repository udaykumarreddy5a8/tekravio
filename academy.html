// The Academy Process

Three steps from scoping
to confident engineers.

No generic curricula. No off-the-shelf content. Every engagement runs through the same three-phase process.

// Step 01
🔍
Skill Gap Assessment
We interview your team leads and review your codebase to understand exactly where capability gaps live. You get a written assessment before any training begins — not a generic needs-analysis form.
// Step 02
🎯
Custom Curriculum Design
We build a programme specifically for your team. Real exercises use your actual tech stack. Case studies come from your domain. Every module maps to an outcome your engineers will use the very next sprint.
// Step 03
⚡
Live, Hands-On Delivery
Senior engineers deliver every session. Live coding, real-time Q&A, and exercises that produce working code — not completed worksheets. Post-training office hours and a reference implementation included.
