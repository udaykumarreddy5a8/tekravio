// Our Process

How every engagement
works at Tekravio

No surprises. Every project follows the same transparent, sprint-based process from day one.

01
Free Discovery Call
// Week 0 · Free · No obligation

We understand your codebase, team dynamics, and goals. We give you an honest assessment of what's possible and what it will cost — before you commit to anything.

02
Fixed Scope Proposal
// Day 3–5 · Written · Zero grey zones

You receive a detailed SOW with exactly what we'll deliver, what's explicitly out of scope, the fixed price, and the delivery timeline. No vagueness. Sign only when it's right.

03
Sprint-Based Delivery
// 2–4 week sprints · Working output every cycle

We work in time-boxed sprints. End of each sprint is a live demo with real, working output — not a status deck. You see progress every two to four weeks.

04
Handover — You Own Everything
// Final week · Full IP transfer · Zero lock-in

Clean code, full documentation, a structured knowledge transfer session. Your team can maintain and extend everything independently from day one.

Uday didn't start Tekravio to build a company. He started it because he was tired of watching enterprises get failed by the firms they trusted most.

After years at Deloitte, Udaykumar Reddy had a front-row seat to how the consulting industry really operates. He saw the patterns that damage enterprise technology programmes — not as an outside observer, but from inside the machine that creates them.

He saw projects staffed with eight people when two great engineers would have delivered better results in half the time. He saw scope written deliberately vague to maximise change request revenue. He saw senior partners win deals they'd never touch again, while junior analysts learned on client time.

"The problem isn't that enterprises don't invest in technology. It's that the firms they hire are structurally incentivised to drag projects, not finish them. Tekravio is built to be the firm I always wished existed on the other side of that table."

— Udaykumar Reddy, Founder, Tekravio
