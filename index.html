// Our Process

How every engagement
works at Tekravio

No surprises. Every project follows the same transparent, sprint-based process from day one.

01
Free Discovery Call
// Week 0 · Free · No obligation
We understand your codebase, team dynamics, and goals. We give you an honest assessment — including what we can't help with. No padded scope, no over-promising.
02
Fixed Scope Proposal
// Day 3–5 · Written · Zero grey zones
You receive a detailed SOW with exactly what we'll deliver, what's explicitly out of scope, a fixed price, and a hard end date. You approve before a single line of work begins.
03
Sprint-Based Delivery
// 2–4 week sprints · Working output every cycle
We work in time-boxed sprints. End of each sprint is a live demo with real, working output — not a status deck. You see tangible progress every fortnight, not after three months.
04
Handover — You Own Everything
// Final week · Full IP transfer · Zero lock-in
Clean code, full documentation, a structured knowledge transfer session. Your team can maintain, extend, and build on everything independently. We don't create dependency — we eliminate it.

💬 Talk to Uday in LinkedIn

Uday didn't start Tekravio to build a company. He started it because he was tired of watching great enterprises get let down by the firms they trusted most.

After years at Deloitte, Udaykumar Reddy had a front-row seat to how the consulting industry really works. He saw smart enterprise clients — across the US, Europe, and India — pay premium fees and receive junior resources, stretched timelines, and deliverables that looked great in a deck but fell apart in production.

He saw projects staffed with eight people when two great engineers would have delivered better work in half the time. He watched accounts managed for billing optimisation, not client outcomes. And he watched clients keep renewing — not because they were satisfied, but because they didn't believe there was a better option available to them.

"The problem isn't that enterprises don't invest in technology. It's that the firms they hire are structurally incentivised to drag projects, not finish them. I built Tekravio to be the firm I always wished existed when I was on the other side of the table."

— Udaykumar Reddy, Founder, Tekravio
