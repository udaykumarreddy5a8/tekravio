// How Every Engagement Works

Four steps from first call
to full handover.

No surprises. Every Studio project follows the same transparent, sprint-based process from day one.

01
Free Discovery Call
// Week 0 · Free · No obligation
We understand your codebase, team dynamics, and goals. You get an honest assessment — including what we can't help with. No padded scope. No over-promising.
02
Fixed Scope Proposal
// Day 3–5 · Written · Zero grey zones
You receive a detailed SOW with exactly what we'll deliver, what's explicitly out of scope, a fixed price, and a hard end date. You approve before a single line of work begins.
03
Sprint-Based Delivery
// 2–4 week sprints · Working output every cycle
We work in time-boxed sprints. End of each sprint is a live demo with real, working output — not a status deck. Tangible progress every fortnight, not after three months.
04
Handover — You Own Everything
// Final week · Full IP transfer · Zero lock-in
Clean code, full documentation, structured knowledge transfer. Your team can maintain and extend everything independently. We eliminate dependency — we don't create it.
