// The Process

How a Studio engagement works

Every project follows the same sprint structure — whether it's a 2-week proof of concept or a 6-month platform build.

01
Discovery Call

30 minutes. We listen to your problem, not pitch our services. You walk away with a clear picture of what's achievable and at what cost.

02
Fixed Proposal

We send a written proposal within 48 hours. Fixed price, defined scope, sprint schedule, and milestone credits clause — all in one document.

03
Sprint Delivery

2-week sprints. Working software at the end of every sprint — not a progress update. You see it running before we move to the next sprint.

04
Handover

Full code handover, documentation, deployment runbook, and knowledge transfer session. You own everything. No lock-in.

🎯
Sprint Credit Guarantee

If we miss an agreed sprint milestone, you get a credit on the next sprint — automatically, without having to ask. Accountability isn't a clause buried in the contract. It's how we work.

🔐
Full IP Ownership

Every line of code, every document, every design — all of it belongs to you at project close. We sign an IP assignment agreement at kickoff. No exceptions.
