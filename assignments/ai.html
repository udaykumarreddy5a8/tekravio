// 01 · The Brief

What You're Building

The AI model landscape is overwhelming. New models launch every week. Enterprise clients ask: "Which AI should we use for our use case?" Most people can't answer this clearly. You will.

Your assignment is to research every major AI model currently available — across all major providers — and produce a clear, structured, honest reference document that maps each model to its specific purpose. Not marketing copy. Not hype. The truth about what each model does best, what it doesn't, and when to use it.

// The Deliverable

A comprehensive AI Model Reference Document — structured, well-researched, and written for an enterprise technology decision-maker (CTO, VP Eng, or Head of L&D) who needs to pick the right AI tool for their problem. Delivered as a PDF or well-structured Markdown document.
