// The Context
Llama 4 Scout is the open-weight model leader in 2026 — and its 10M token context window is its most distinctive feature. No commercial frontier model currently offers more than 2M tokens. Meta's Apache 2.0 license makes this completely free to use, modify, fine-tune, and distribute commercially.

At 2,600 tokens per second, Llama 4 Scout is dramatically faster than commercial APIs (typically 50–200 t/s). This speed + open weights combination unlocks applications that are impractical with commercial APIs: ultra-low-latency production inference, fully offline deployments, and real-time applications at scale without per-token costs. Meta's bet: democratise frontier-adjacent AI to build the largest possible developer ecosystem.

01
The 10M Token Context Window — What It Actually Enables
// 22 marks
▼

10 million tokens is more than GPT-5.5 (1.1M), Gemini 3.1 Pro (1M), and Fable 5 (1M) combined. Document what this enables — and what the limits are.

10M tokens in practical terms: Convert 10 million tokens into practical equivalents: pages of A4 text, hours of audio transcripts, lines of code, number of full books, and hours of subtitled video. What entire workflows become single-prompt tasks that previously required chunking, summarisation pipelines, or multi-pass processing?
Attention quality at extreme lengths: Does attention degrade across 10M tokens? What is known about Llama 4 Scout's recall accuracy ('needle-in-a-haystack' performance) at 1M, 5M, and 10M token positions? Compare to Claude Fable 5's performance on long-context recall tasks at its 1M limit.
Why commercial models cap at 1–2M: Commercial models like GPT-5.5 and Claude Fable 5 cap at 1–1.1M despite the hardware existing to go further. Document the four reasons: pricing model complexity, hallucination risk at extreme lengths, inference cost, and the limited number of enterprise customers who actually need more than 1M tokens. Why hasn't Anthropic or OpenAI offered 10M?
Use cases genuinely requiring 10M: Three specific enterprise use cases where 10M tokens is genuinely needed — not just nice-to-have: (a) full codebase analysis without chunking (describe a specific repository size), (b) entire legal case history in one prompt (describe the legal scenario), (c) year-long meeting transcript analysis. For each, explain precisely why the task breaks at 1M tokens.
The real limit of 10M context: 10M context does not mean perfect performance across 10M tokens. Document the known limitations: attention degradation, 'lost in the middle' effects, increased hallucination risk at extreme lengths. For which tasks does 10M context create genuine value vs which tasks degrade in quality?

Apache 2.0 License & Meta's Open-Source Strategy

▼

The Llama developer ecosystem: Llama has the largest open-weight AI developer ecosystem. Document: Ollama integration, vLLM support, HuggingFace community, academic usage. Answer in Your README

Four questions requiring specific evidence and calculations.

Q1
A startup wants to process entire GitHub repositories (averaging 800K tokens per repo) through AI for security vulnerability scanning. They need to process 10,000 repos per month. Design the Llama 4 Scout self-hosted architecture. Calculate: total GPU hours needed, monthly infrastructure cost, and cost-per-repo. Compare to using Claude Fable 5 API for the same workload.
Q2
Llama 4 Scout achieves 2,600 t/s on optimised hardware. A real-time coding assistant needs responses in under 2 seconds. At 200 output tokens per response, what throughput is required? Can Llama 4 Scout serve this with a single GPU instance, or do you need multiple? Design the infrastructure for 100 concurrent developers.
Q3
A company wants to fine-tune Llama 4 Scout on 5 years of their proprietary customer service conversations (50GB of text, approximately 20B tokens). What hardware is required? What is the training cost on cloud GPUs? How does the resulting fine-tuned model compare in cost and capability to prompt-engineering a commercial model like GPT-5.5?
Q4
Apache 2.0 allows commercial use of Llama 4 Scout without paying Meta. But could Meta change the license on future Llama versions? Research Meta's license history across Llama 1, 2, 3, and 4. Has it become more or less restrictive over time? How should a company building a product on Llama 4 Scout plan for future license changes?
