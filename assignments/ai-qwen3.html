// The Context
The Qwen 3.x family from Alibaba Cloud is the most multilingual frontier model family in the world — 201 languages, Apache 2.0 license, and prices ranging from $0.10/M (Qwen 3 9B) to $1.25/M (Qwen3.7 Max). Qwen3.7 Max is the cheapest model in the top-10 by GPQA Diamond score.

Qwen 3 235B achieves 85.7% on AIME '24 (a math competition benchmark). The unique Qwen 3.6-Flash introduces Linear-Attention Context Windows that support 1M tokens with near-zero latency degradation — a technical achievement that standard quadratic attention cannot match at that scale. For Tekravio's planned international expansion, Qwen is the most important non-Western frontier model to understand.

01
The Complete Qwen 3.x Family Map
// 20 marks
▼
