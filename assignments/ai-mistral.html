// The Context
Mistral AI was founded in Paris in 2023 with a founding thesis of EU AI sovereignty: Europe needs a frontier AI lab that operates under European law, stores data in Europe, and is not subject to US government data access laws (CLOUD Act). By 2026, Mistral has shipped three 2026-generation models: Devstral 2 (agentic coding model), Mistral Small 4 (6B active parameters for lightweight deployment), and Mistral Large 3 (80+ languages for multilingual European enterprise).

The EU AI Act entered into force in 2025. Understanding Mistral's position requires understanding this regulatory context — which is the focus of Module 2. For Tekravio's planned German market entry, this assignment is directly actionable.

02
GDPR, EU AI Act & the Compliance Advantage
// 22 marks
▼

This is the most important module for Tekravio's European expansion. No US-centric AI research covers this adequately. Understand it completely.

GDPR articles that apply to AI model usage: Document the specific GDPR articles that apply when processing EU personal data through AI models: Article 28 (Data Processing Agreements — required when a processor handles data), Chapter V (transfers outside the EU — applies when data goes to US servers), and Article 22 (automated individual decision-making — applies when AI makes consequential decisions). For each article: what obligation does it create, and how do US-based providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) comply or fail to comply with it?
EU AI Act (in force 2025): The EU AI Act entered into force in 2025. Document the four risk categories: prohibited AI systems, high-risk AI systems, limited-risk systems, and minimal-risk systems. Where do GPT-5.5, Claude Fable 5, and Gemini 3.1 Pro fall when used for enterprise applications? What specific obligations does deploying a 'high-risk AI system' impose on the deployer (not the provider)?
Mistral's specific EU compliance claims: Mistral claims to be GDPR-compliant by design. Document exactly what Mistral offers vs what Anthropic and OpenAI offer EU customers: EU data residency (are data centres in the EU?), standard contractual clauses in DPAs, data processing agreement terms, and data retention policies. Compare claim by claim.
DSGVO — German GDPR enforcement: German data protection authorities (Datenschutzbehörden) are among the strictest GDPR enforcers in Europe. Research and document one real case where a German data protection authority investigated or took action against an AI provider or a company using AI to process German personal data. What was the outcome? What architectural decision would have prevented the investigation?
Tekravio Germany market compliance architecture: Tekravio is targeting the German market. For a German healthcare client (governed by DSGVO + HIPAA for US operations + German BDSG health data regulations), which AI provider is compliant for processing patient data? Build a compliance decision flowchart: given data type and country, which model provider is compliant.

// 02 · Design Questions
Answer in Your README

Four questions. Module 2 depth required for Questions 1 and 2.

Q1
A German e-commerce company wants to use AI for automated customer service responses that reference purchase history, shipping addresses, and payment information. Under GDPR Article 28, they need a Data Processing Agreement. Which AI providers can provide a GDPR-compliant DPA that allows this use case? Does Claude Fable 5 qualify? Does GPT-5.5? Does Mistral Large 3 via La Plateforme? Research the current DPA terms for each.
Q2
The EU AI Act classifies AI systems by risk. A Tekravio Studio client wants to build an AI-powered recruitment screening tool that filters CVs and ranks candidates. What risk category does this application fall into under the EU AI Act? What obligations does this create for the client? Which AI model provider best supports compliance with these obligations?
Q3
Devstral 2 scores below Claude Fable 5 on SWE-bench Verified. A EU-based software company argues: 'We should use Devstral 2 because it keeps our code on EU servers, even if the quality is slightly lower.' How would you quantify 'slightly lower'? At what quality gap does GDPR compliance stop being worth the capability trade-off? Build the calculation for a code-review application processing 10,000 pull requests per month.
Q4
Tekravio is planning a German university partnership. The university's legal team asks: 'Can you guarantee that student data will not leave the EU if we use your Academy platform?' Design the full compliance answer: which AI models are used, where is data processed, what DPAs are in place, and what happens if Tekravio's primary AI provider (Anthropic) moves data to US servers?
